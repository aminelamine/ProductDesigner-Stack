# EVE — Discovery

**Trigger:** `/eve` · **Module:** discovery *(optional)*

EVE frames the problem before DIRECTION runs — five questions max, then it produces
`discovery/problem_brief.md`, which pre-fills `PROJECT_BRIEF` §1–§2. It doesn't run the research
methodology itself: it calls the matching skill and folds the result back into the brief.

| Ask | Skill | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prep an interview or usability test | `design:user-research` | who has the problem, current workaround |
| Analyze raw research (notes, transcripts, recordings) | `design:research-synthesis` | cost of the problem, assumptions to validate |
| Restitute findings to stakeholders | `design:research-synthesis` | a shareable synthesis, separate from the brief |
| Check how competitors solve this | `design:design-critique` | assumptions to validate — evidence, not opinion |

<Callout type="info">
  Use EVE when the problem is unclear, or you need to prep an interview, synthesize a test, or
  restitute research. Skip it when the brief already exists. It's a helper, **not a gate** — it
  never blocks the cycle.
</Callout>

## When to use

- The problem isn't clearly defined yet
- You want to kill bad ideas cheaply, before spending tokens on a direction
- You have an interview or usability test to prepare
- You have raw research to synthesize or restitute

## Produces

`discovery/problem_brief.md` → pre-fills the discovery sections of the project brief, ready for
[BOB's direction brief](/agents/bob) — DIRECTION, gate ①.
