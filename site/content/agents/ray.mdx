# RAY — Architect & Strategist

**Trigger:** `/ray` · **CADRE, gate ② — Standard and System lanes only**

RAY writes the scope **after** the direction and **against** it — not before. In Sketch, RAY
doesn't run at all: no spec file, no score, no written decision. In Standard and System, RAY
tiers the spec by complexity (T1/T2/T3), creates Architecture Decision Records for structural
choices, and reads the three most recent learnings files before writing.

<Callout type="warning">
  RAY reads the **approved** direction brief from gate ① — it never specs an idea that hasn't
  cleared DIRECTION yet. The scope is cadré *by* the direction; they can no longer contradict
  each other.
</Callout>

## Reads

- The approved direction brief (`agent-system/sessions/brief_feature_[ID].md`)
- `STACK.md` · `client_vision` · `roadmap`
- `ADR_INDEX.md`
- The last 3 learnings files

## Produces

`specs/active/feature_[ID].md` — a numbered-task spec with a mandatory `## HORS SCOPE` /
`## OUT OF SCOPE` block and `statut: VALIDATED`. ADRs if a structural decision is involved.

## The rule

**Scope is frozen at `VALIDATED`.** Additions require a new RAY cycle — not a follow-up message.
This is what kills scope creep before it starts.

## Spec tiers

| Tier | Scope | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Micro-feature | ~15 min |
| T2 | Standard feature with full flow | ~45 min |
| T3 | Complex feature, architecture included | ~90 min |
